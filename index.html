Quest loop1) Collect 5 crystals   2) Defeat 6 minions   3) Touch the altar to open the gate   4) Defeat the boss. Then you win — and get 100 bonus coins.
On QuestLeft thumbstick to move, right thumbstick to snap-turn. Right trigger = sword swing. Left trigger = magic bolt. Walk into a portal in the Rec Center to start that quest. Touch a red heart to heal, a gold coin to collect it.
On desktopClick the scene to capture the mouse, WASD to move, mouse to look, click = sword, F = magic bolt, H = heal, E = use altar. Q / E also turn if the mouse isn't captured.
Party playOne player hosts, the others join with the code. The host runs the enemies and quest progress, everyone sees each other's avatars. A normal home Wi-Fi WebRTC connection works.
